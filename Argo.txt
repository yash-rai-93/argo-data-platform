PROJECT NAME: ARGO

Product Intelligence, Growth Analytics & Decision Intelligence Platform

============================================================
MISSION
=======

Design and build ARGO as if it were an internal analytics and decision intelligence platform used by a rapidly growing SaaS company with tens of thousands of users, multiple product teams, executive stakeholders, customer success teams, growth teams, sales teams, finance teams, and data teams.

ARGO is NOT a dashboard project.

ARGO is NOT a churn prediction project.

ARGO is NOT a cohort analysis project.

ARGO is a complete Product Intelligence Platform whose purpose is to convert raw product activity into business decisions.

The system must resemble capabilities found across:

* Amplitude
* Mixpanel
* HubSpot
* Gainsight
* Pendo
* FullStory
* Google Analytics
* Looker
* Snowflake
* Segment

while remaining achievable for an individual portfolio project.

============================================================
CORE BUSINESS PROBLEM
=====================

Modern SaaS companies generate enormous amounts of behavioral data but struggle to answer critical questions:

Growth Questions

* What drives retention?
* What drives churn?
* What drives activation?
* What drives upgrades?

Product Questions

* Which features matter?
* Which features are ignored?
* Which features predict long-term retention?

Executive Questions

* Why is growth slowing?
* What customer segments are healthiest?
* Where should resources be invested?

Customer Success Questions

* Which customers are at risk?
* Which customers need intervention?
* Which accounts are expansion opportunities?

ARGO must answer these questions through data.
